Ingredients & Preparation Notes

Dishwashing Liquid

  • 300ml (1¼ cups) boiling water + 1 tbsp washing soda crystals
  • 100ml (½ cup) liquid castile soap + ½ tsp glycerine
  • Mixed in a heatproof bowl and bottled with a glass pump

Powdered Soap

  • 2 cups washing soda
  • 2 cups baking soda
  • 2 cups borax
  • 1 grated bar of Castile soap or all-natural soap

Sanitizer Blend

  • ⅔ cup rubbing alcohol
  • ⅓ cup aloe vera
  • 5–10 drops of essential oil (optional)

Bleach Agents

  • Arbutin
  • Kojic acid
  • Glutathione
