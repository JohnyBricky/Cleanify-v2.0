About Cleanify

Cleanify is built for people with tight schedules or busy households who need reliable help keeping their indoor and outdoor spaces clean, fresh, and organized—without the stress.

Since 2022

Company History

Cleanify was established in 2022 by four students from the University of Makati who stepped up to help families and businesses keep their spaces sanitized during the pandemic. What started as a student-led initiative quickly grew through word-of-mouth, media features, and major institutional contracts. Today, led by Founder John Bricks F. Ireneo—overseeing operations, manpower, technical systems, and outreach—the CleanTeam continues delivering dependable service to satisfied customers across the metro.
